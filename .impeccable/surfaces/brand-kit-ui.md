# Learli brand-kit adoption

Targets: src/app, src/features, src/components
Modes: public discovery Persuade/Operate; accounts and workspaces Operate;
policy explanations Read.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Learli makes a tutor and a real lesson time easy to find, then keeps
coordination clear. The existing working marketplace supplies the content.

OWN-WORLD: The owner's brand kit 2.0 fixes the direction: paper, white and navy,
Lexend interface, Literata reading, a mint phrase highlight, deep-teal links,
original wordmark and flat bordered 16px panels. No invented photo material.

STORY: Understand one-to-one external tutoring, inspect the actual offer, then
choose the available action. Empty, unavailable and synthetic states stay honest.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Public home puts a large left-aligned navy headline beside the
real finder or honest availability state on desktop and stacks on mobile.
Workspaces lead with the task and current lesson/state; forms group context,
inputs and one primary action. The header carries original artwork and usable
navigation. No content hides behind animation.

FORM: Direct implementation of user-selected kit boards 08-interface and 09-in-use;
no random direction seed or generated composition replaces the specified identity.
Code-first for this session from supplied boards; no standing workflow default
is inferred or stored. Server behavior and native interactions are preserved.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Only supplied SVGs ship. No raster production is planned. Product truth remains
in canonical context; example LMS features and unsupported claims are excluded.
