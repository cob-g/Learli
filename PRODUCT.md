# Product

This is an Impeccable reference derived from the existing approved project
context and the user's 2026-10-13 brand-kit adoption request. The canonical
product authority remains `context/project-overview.md`, `context/prd.md` and
`context/architecture.md`; this file does not approve new capabilities or policies.

## Platform

web

## Users

Parents and guardians coordinate tutoring for authorized linked learners;
eligible independent learners, including College Support, manage their own
lessons. Tutors manage their offer and schedule. Administrators carry out scoped
eligibility, support and safety tasks.

## Product Purpose

Find a suitable academic tutor, choose a real available time, and coordinate a
one-to-one lesson delivered independently through Zoom or Google Meet.

## Positioning

Learli is a tutoring marketplace and booking intermediary. The product brings
discovery and lesson coordination together; instruction stays with the tutor.

## Operating Context

Responsive Next.js web application, native form controls and accessible keyboard
navigation. Development uses synthetic identities with separate development/test
data. Private workspaces retain role and record-ownership checks. Times include
the viewer's date and time-zone context.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Discovery, rule-based matching, account access, authorized learner relationships,
tutor schedules, bookings, private lesson links and scoped support already exist.
No LMS, practice sets, academic progress dashboard, direct messages, built-in
classroom, invented reviews or invented availability. Payments remain paused.
Real-user eligibility, safeguarding, policy activation and external production
operations remain subject to their recorded gates.

## Brand Commitments

Use the owner-supplied Learli brand kit2.0; lowercase artwork, Learli in ordinary
prose, plain specific language. The user requested applying this kit across the
current frontend with Impeccable and build-feature. Visual details belong to
`context/ui-context.md` and `DESIGN.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

The kit provides original SVG logos, guidelines and design tokens. Its photo
frames are briefs only. No rights-cleared photography is supplied. Example
practice-set/message screens are visual illustrations, not approved functionality.
Current queries and synthetic fixtures provide truth for development screens;
never promote these to genuine customer claims.

## Product Principles

- Make the next useful action easy to find.
- Show real current state and truthful recovery paths.
- Preserve privacy, guardian controls and booking integrity.
- Keep instruction outside the coordination product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG2.2AA: readable contrast, keyboard operation, named form controls,
visible focus, understandable errors, reduced motion, responsive layouts and
text enlargement. Preserve all four learner levels, including College Support.
