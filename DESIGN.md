# Design System: Learli

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Paper, navy and one useful signal"**

Learli's implemented brand kit 2.0 combines a paper ground, white working surfaces,
navy hierarchy and a restrained mint phrase highlight. Lexend makes the interface
direct and readable; Literata gives longer explanations and tutor biographies a
distinct reading voice. The original wordmark supplies the identity rather than
a newly drawn logo or decorative imagery.

The same flat materials carry public discovery, account forms and private lesson
coordination. Borders and spacing group work; native controls expose real choices
and current state. Synthetic testing notices, unavailable states and policy draft
labels remain visible. This is a derived reference for the current implementation,
not evidence of release, completed browser verification or real-user readiness.
Canonical authority remains [UI context](context/ui-context.md) and the product,
architecture and PRD documents referenced by [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).

**Key Characteristics:**

- Paper and white surfaces with navy hierarchy.
- Lexend interface and explicit Literata reading passages.
- Flat bordered panels and native accessible controls.
- Mint highlights and small teal selection signals.
- Truthful states, readable lesson details and responsive wrapping.

## Colors

The palette pairs cool navy ink with warm green neutrals; the frontmatter resolves
the actual CSS aliases without introducing another theme.

### Primary

- **Navy:** Headings, primary actions, current navigation and selected choices.
  Its hover shade is darker; the pressed primary action uses Ink.

### Secondary

- **Bright teal:** Original artwork and small selected-state dots; the inverse
  action uses it as a fill with Ink text.
- **Mint:** The home headline's phrase highlight, initials avatar fills and the
  owner-authorized sign-in companion's italic emphasis and category ribbon.
- **Deep teal:** Underlined links, success text and focus on light surfaces.

### Neutral

- **Paper / White / Mist:** Page ground, working surfaces and neutral insets or hover.
- **Ink / Slate:** Body text and secondary text. Slate also defines enabled fields.
- **Stone:** Panel borders and disabled select boundaries.
- **White / Stone on navy:** Inverse foreground and secondary inverse text.

### Semantic states

Success uses Deep teal on Mist. Warning retains its amber foreground and warm
background. Error uses Alert with the pale error background where a notice needs
a fill. Status text and functional icons communicate meaning alongside color.
The scrim is a translucent navy-toned native-dialog backdrop, not a brand tint.

**The Readable Signal Rule.** Bright teal and Mint are fills or accents on light
surfaces; body text and links use Ink, Navy, Slate or Deep teal. Never put white
text on Bright teal.

**The Surface Focus Rule.** Focus is a two-pixel outline with a two-pixel offset;
light surfaces use Deep teal, navy fields and checked matching choices use White.
Text selection also reverses between navy-on-white and white-on-navy contexts.

The sidecar's synthesized tonal ramps are preview strips only. They do not add
production colors or authorize gradients, tints or recoloring the supplied artwork.

## Typography

**Display and interface font:** Lexend, with Avenir Next, Verdana and sans-serif
fallbacks. **Reading font:** Literata, with Georgia and serif fallbacks.

Both are configured in `src/app/layout.tsx` with `next/font/google`: Lexend
normal weights (400/500/600), Literata normal and italic (400/500), display swap.
Next serves the generated font assets from the application; Literata is not
preloaded. Font synthesis is disabled. Labels and numbers use the interface font.

### Hierarchy

Shared display and headline are the two fluid roles. Page, section and item titles give
task pages a stable hierarchy; lead is the larger introductory interface copy.
Body and prose share the same Lexend role. Reading explicitly combines Literata
with the reading size. Action and time share the stronger interface treatment;
label, label-strong, small and caption provide secondary hierarchy. Exact sizes,
weights, line heights and tracking are in the frontmatter.

**The Two Voices Rule.** Use Lexend for headings, forms, names, dates and lesson
times. Apply Literata explicitly to longer explanations or biographies; the
`prose` size token alone does not switch font family.

The owner-authorized sign-in exception adds local title, narrow-title, companion
display and medium-weight action roles, recorded with the `sign-in` prefix.
Only the companion's final emphasized word uses Literata italic (400), with
tracking (-0.01em). Its owner-supplied introductory labels stay local content;
they do not establish a reusable eyebrow or heading convention.

The Create account exception adds its own centered title, narrow-title and
medium-weight action roles under the `sign-up` prefix. These share values with
sign-in where the source does, without changing the shared hierarchy. The
reference's “Get started” introductory label is local content, not a reusable
eyebrow token or a new heading convention.

Sentence case, left alignment and normal label tracking are the defaults. The
prose container is (34rem); actual measure also follows its available column.
Headings and important identifiers wrap. Directory biography excerpts are the
observed exception: they clamp to two lines before the full profile.

## Layout

The page shell is centered and full width up to (80rem), with horizontal gutters
of (1rem), then (1.5rem) from the small breakpoint and (2rem) from large. Standard
page spacing is (2rem) vertically, increasing to (3rem) at medium. Core spacing
steps are in the frontmatter; larger public sections also use (6rem).

Public home stacks introduction and finder, then uses two flexible columns at
large widths. Default auth content centers at (36rem). Matching questions constrain the
working column to (42rem). Directory filters become a (18rem) sidebar at large;
dedicated workspaces use a sidebar of min(17rem, 30vw). Tutor and matching result
rows change columns at a container width of (48rem), rather than assuming that
the page viewport guarantees room inside a sidebar layout.

Headers and action groups wrap as content grows. Main content columns and fields
have zero minimum inline size where needed; fieldsets and legends stay within
their container. Actions allow their labels to wrap. Native time-zone and subject
selects occupy the available width. Lesson dates, times and zones remain visible
and wrap instead of being shortened. Narrow layouts stack sections; bounded
scroll regions are used for wide administrative tables.

The public/auth desktop header has its own wider bounded row from (64rem):
maximum width (90rem), horizontal gutters (2rem), minimum height (4.5rem) and
three grid columns in a (1 : 2.25 : 1) ratio with (1.5rem) gaps. Its navigation
capsule is centered independently of account state. These are minimum sizes,
not content caps. Within a desktop header container no wider than (50rem),
the account occupies a full second row and its signed-in trigger uses the
surface radius, giving enlarged names room to wrap. Ordinary desktop geometry
keeps the account in the right column.

Only `/sign-in` uses the owner-supplied unboxed composition. Its outer grid
caps at (90rem), with (1rem) inset and gap, and columns in a (1 : 1.08) ratio.
The left column has (2.5rem) horizontal padding; a vertically centered working
column caps at (26.25rem / 420px), with (3rem) block padding. The layout's
viewport-based minimum height is not a crop: notices, errors and selected-booking
details expand normal document flow, and the compact footer shared with Create
account follows.
At a sign-in container width of (56.25rem / 900px) or less, the companion hides,
the outer inset disappears and the left gutter becomes (1.25rem). At (24rem /
384px) or less, the recovery link moves below the password input. These local
container queries do not change the navbar or other auth screens.

Only `/sign-up` uses the separate centered reference card. Its normal-flow stage
has minimum height `calc(100svh - 4.5625rem)` and padding (4rem 1.25rem 6rem).
The White card caps at (32.5rem / 520px); its exact padding is recorded above.
Native account-intent cards occupy two columns, with Choose later spanning both.
At a sign-up container width of (73.75rem / 1180px) or less, floating subject
groups and small dots hide; the orb moves to the right gutter. At (56.25rem /
900px) or less, stage padding becomes (1.5rem 1.25rem 4rem), card padding becomes
(1.75rem 1.25rem), and the card loses its border, corner rounding, White fill and
shadow. The orb hides. At (24rem / 384px) or less, intentions
stack in one column and the card's horizontal padding disappears. Notices,
pending selection and the full form remain in document flow; the compact footer
shared with Sign in follows. These local container queries do not change sign-in or
the navbar.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from White against Paper or Mist, thin borders and spacing. The
implemented floating and modal shadow tokens are `none`. The native confirmation
dialog uses a dimmed scrim, not a cast shadow. Content appears
immediately. Shared actions use a short color transition (150ms); pending spinners
are limited by motion-safe or reduced-motion utilities where used. The declared
enter/exit curves are retained in the sidecar as source tokens, not evidence that
an entrance animation runs on every surface.

**The Flat Surface Rule.** Group work with borders, tonal surfaces and spacing;
do not add resting card shadows, glass or decorative elevation outside the
documented owner-authorized exceptions below.

### Public home motion

The public home page has authored content motion (`home.css`,
`HomeMotion`). Four moves: the mint highlight is drawn across a headline phrase
(760ms); hairlines above list rows are ruled left to right (900ms, staggered
70ms, capped); a blue stroke is drawn through the three lesson steps (1100ms)
behind their glass tiles; and each lesson glyph assembles as the stroke
reaches it (900ms, 250ms apart). All use `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
Hover and focus sweep the highlight across an index row (320ms).

The sample review carousel (preview environments only) swings three pictures
round a shallow 3D arc with an overshoot, `cubic-bezier(0.4, 2, 0.3, 1)` over
800ms, and brings each quote in word by word (220ms per word, 25ms apart). Within
the home page, it is the one component that advances by itself: every six
seconds, waiting under a pointer or keyboard focus, and never under reduced
motion. Its one control is Next. The briefly running decoration on sign-in and Create
account is a separate local exception described below.

**The Complete Markup Rule.** Motion only withholds a decorative stroke that is
still below the viewport. Text, links and final arrangements are in the server
markup; reduced motion keeps each state and removes the travel.

### Public home liquid glass

On the public home page and the small-screen public/auth header and dock, the
flat surface rule gives way to one material (in `site-shell.css`),
`.glass`: a softened and tinted backdrop, a lit rim, a soft offset contact
shadow and a glint that follows the pointer. It is used for floating layers
only: the small-screen header and dock, the finder, the lesson tiles, the card
for what Learli looks after, the sample-review sheet and the primary and outline actions (300ms scale
on hover). Light sheets carry
a white tint of 62%; sheets on navy carry 9% and keep white text. Chromium also
bends the backdrop through an SVG filter. Reduced transparency, higher contrast,
forced colors and browsers without backdrop filters get solid surfaces.

The owner-authorized desktop navbar is a narrower exception to that material:
a pale Paper sheet (86% opacity) with saturation (140%), blur (12px) and a
one-pixel Navy bottom edge (8%). It is present before and after scrolling,
without the liquid rim or glint. Its White navigation capsule has a restrained
contact shadow (`0 1px 2px` at Ink 4%) and a Navy/White border (16%). Solid
White and system-color fallbacks preserve reduced-transparency, contrast and
forced-color preferences. This treatment is local to the public/auth desktop
navbar; it does not replace the other glass or private workspace surfaces.

**The Something Behind It Rule.** A sheet always lies over Learli's own
material (photographs, the illustration, tonal rings, the scrolling page) and
its text holds AA over all of it. Glass is never laid on an empty ground.

### Sign-in reference exception

The sign-in companion uses solid Navy, a diagonal Mint ribbon and a Bright teal
orb with a Paper rim. The local submit action alone carries a small Navy shadow
(`0 0.5rem 1.25rem -0.625rem`, Navy 60% against transparency); this does not add
a shared card or button shadow. Its enabled hover lifts (1px) and softens the
shadow (`0 0.875rem 1.625rem -0.75rem`, Navy 70% against transparency), while
the arrow advances (4px); press scales to (0.985). Transform and shadow use
(200ms), and the arrow uses (300ms), with `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`.
Background color changes immediately, including across disabled/enabled states.

Two identical groups in each ribbon track include their boundary gap, so a
linear translation of half the track repeats seamlessly. The first row retains
a (38s) period, the second reverses over (46s); the orb alternates upward (8px)
over (6s) with the same local easing. CSS starts every animation paused. A small
sign-in-only client companion runs them only after hydration with observer
support, while the panel intersects the viewport, the document is visible and
its completion latch is unset. A (4500ms) timer in the mount effect sets that
latch and freezes decoration in place; elapsed time includes hidden/offscreen
time, and later visibility changes cannot restart it. There is no decorative
motion button. The timer, observer and visibility subscription clean up on
unmount; no credential or authentication state enters this controller.

Without scripts or observer support, the complete artwork stays still. Reduced
motion defines no loop and removes local transitions, including the field
border's (150ms linear) transition. The narrow-layout rule
hides the companion; forced colors also hides it, keeps a single working column
and removes the submit shadow. This controlled decorative motion stays local
to `/sign-in`.

### Create account reference exception

The signup-only card carries a soft two-part shadow: a small Ink contact and a
diffuse Navy cast. Floating academic chips, category mini-panels and the teal
orb have their own local shadows; exact source expressions are recorded in the
sidecar. These materials do not replace the shared flat panel or button rules.
A quiet radial dot field sits behind the card. The angled Mint/Navy band is
static; it has no drift or background transition.

The enabled Navy submit uses (200ms) transform/shadow feedback, a (1px) lift,
(4px) arrow travel over (300ms), and (0.985) press scale with
`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`. Its background changes immediately. Two
academic-label groups retain an alternating (7s) bob upward (8px); the right
group starts at (-3s). The orb uses the same travel and easing over (6s). The
controller freezes these local signup animations within (4500ms) of mounting,
as described below.

Native signup inputs transition their border over (150ms linear) and focus
shadow over (200ms) with the local easing. Native account choices use the same
border/shadow timing plus a (150ms linear) selected-background transition.
These transitions exist only when motion is allowed. A disabled fieldset clears
input, choice, submit and arrow transitions/transforms immediately. During the
real form's `aria-busy="true"` state, the decorative Chevron hides and a small
open ring replaces it; only the ring rotates, over (800ms linear) indefinitely
while busy. Reduced motion keeps the static ring and existing Please wait text.

CSS starts artwork animations paused. The signup artwork controller adds its
running attribute only after hydration with observer support, while the artwork
intersects the viewport, the document is visible and its completion latch is
unset. A (4500ms) timer in the mount effect sets that latch and freezes decoration
in place; elapsed time includes hidden/offscreen time, and later visibility
changes cannot restart it. There is no decorative motion button. The timer,
observer and visibility subscription clean up on unmount. No script or missing
observer support leaves the artwork still. Reduced motion defines no artwork
loops or pending rotation and removes local transitions and submit/arrow travel.
Forced colors hides the artwork, removes card/submit shadows and restores
system-color control borders. These rules stay local to `/sign-up`.

## Shapes

Controls use gently rounded corners; panels are rounder and native confirmation
dialogs slightly rounder again. Small tags and checkboxes have their own compact
radii, all recorded above. Circular shapes are used for initials avatars and
small active dots. Matching choices currently use the control radius, not pills.
Thin Stone borders separate panels and list rows; enabled fields use Slate.
Original logo geometry is not modified.

The sign-in exception has local control and companion radii, recorded with the
`sign-in` prefix. Its fields and submit use the former; its solid decorative
panel uses the latter. These values do not replace shared control or panel
radii. Capsule labels on the ribbon are decoration, not actionable choice chips.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use the shared semantic colors and type roles from the implemented CSS.
- **Do** preserve original wordmarks and the icon, with reverse artwork on Navy.
- **Do** keep native controls, visible labels, focus and truthful state text.
- **Do** let actions, names, lesson times and time-zone context wrap on narrow or enlarged layouts.
- **Do** use borders and spacing to group work on White and Paper surfaces.
- **Do** keep the desktop public/auth navbar compact, with its original SVG, centered White capsule, light Mint hover/current fills, full names and visible focus.
- **Do** keep the sign-in reference's local sizes, briefly running decoration and unboxed form scoped to `/sign-in`, preserving the 4500ms settling bound, real form states, static fallbacks and other auth frames.
- **Do** keep the Create account reference's card, abstract academic decoration and local feedback scoped to `/sign-up`, preserving the 4500ms artwork settling bound, all three native intentions and real form states.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use Bright teal or Mint as ordinary text on a light surface.
- **Don't** add card shadows, glass, decorative gradients or invented imagery outside the public home page and shared header, apart from the documented local sign-in submit shadow and Create account reference's card/artwork/submit shadows and dot field; glass remains for floating layers and photographs stay in the hero, illustrative and uncaptioned.
- **Don't** animate outside the public home page and shared header/footer beyond short state transitions and the documented local sign-in and Create account motion; never hide content behind animation.
- **Don't** turn kit example practice sets, messages or progress screens into product functionality.
- **Don't** invent tutors, availability, reviews, prices or customer claims to fill a layout.
- **Don't** hide synthetic notices, policy drafts, private-data boundaries or booking state for visual polish.
- **Don't** add underlines to desktop public/auth navbar links or apply its compact sizing and pale sheet to mobile or private workspace navigation.
