# Home page photographs

Illustrative photographs for the public home page hero, added on 2026-10-13 at
the owner's request ("add images … of students in the hero section", then "I
want the image look HD and in 3D"). See
`docs/decisions/2026-10-13-home-motion-illustration.md`.

**They are AI-generated.** No real person was photographed. They do not show
actual Learli learners, tutors or lessons, and must never be captioned, credited
or described as if they did. They are stand-ins: replace them with consented
photographs of real lessons when the owner has them, which is what the brand
kit's imagery board asks for.

face is in frame. They sit beside the sample reviews on the home page:

- `moment-desk.webp`: from the original desk photograph (hands writing, a mug,
  books)
- `moment-kitchen.webp`: from the kitchen photograph (a hand with a pen on a
  notebook)
- `moment-problem.webp`: from the worked-problem photograph

account. The two desk layers were made from one original photograph used as a

`tutor-desk.webp` and `study-desk.webp` were added on 2026-10-14 for the Become
a tutor and Subjects pages, when the owner asked for landing-style content on
the pages in the navigation. The still life shows no person. The man in
`tutor-desk.webp` is generated: he is nobody, he is not a Learli tutor, and he
must never be named, captioned or shown anywhere a real tutor is listed.

`family-table.webp` is not a hero picture. It sits between the two lists in
"Learli arranges the lesson", because the owner asked that the section not
reuse a hero photograph. It shows a generated adult and child; like the rest,
they are nobody, and must never be captioned or described as a Learli family.

The two desk layers are the planes of the home hero's layered scene. The cutout
was checked on a contrasting ground for halos and for real transparency before
use. Replacing the photograph means replacing both layers together.

Art direction followed the kit's imagery board: daylight from a window, eyes on
the work rather than the camera, brand colour from objects in the room, no tint
or overlay. Converted with `cwebp` at quality 86 to 90 and served at quality 90.
The laptop screens face away from the camera, so no meeting software, tutor or
lesson content is depicted.
