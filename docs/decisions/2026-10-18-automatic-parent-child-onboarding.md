# Automatic parent-managed child profiles

Status: **AGREED — explicit owner instruction, 2026-10-18.**

An active Parent with verified email can add a new private child profile by
entering minimal details, selecting education level/optional grade and explicitly
confirming parent/authorized-guardian authority and consent to manage the profile
and arrange tutoring. Save immediately establishes the authorized relationship;
the child appears in My Children, Parent Dashboard and booking selectors. Routine
Admin approval is not required for creation, additional children, ordinary profile
or grade updates, or a valid Parent booking for that child.

Email verification proves control of an email address, not guardianship. The
declaration is recorded truthfully as a parent attestation, never staff review or
identity evidence. It grants no access to an existing student account. Secure
existing-account linking, disputed guardianship and suspicious access remain
separate exceptional workflows. No claim by name/email is permitted.

For ordinary local operation, introduce immutable guardian admission-v2 and
parent booking-v2 receipts without changing historical v1 receipts or heads.
New Parent bookings are atomically confirmed under the existing no-money local
timing settings after current ownership, tutor eligibility, schedule and conflict
checks. Historical pending requests remain pending; independent College and old
bookings retain their original rules. Existing scoped exceptional restriction and
revocation, private meeting links and assigned-Tutor completion remain enforced.

This supersedes routine new-child/Parent-booking review language in the Oct18
ordinary-local decision for this slice only. It does not authorize account claims,
tutor autoapproval, data cleanup, cloud deployment, payments or public-minor
launch. Market-specific consent/legal and actual safeguarding launch gates remain.
