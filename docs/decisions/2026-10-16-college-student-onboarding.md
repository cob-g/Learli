# College Student onboarding and role-specific booking views

- Date: 2026-10-16, Asia/Manila.
- Owner approved the Student cross-check recommendation: “ok approve go for
  recommendation”. Software scope is a clear College Student entry, correction
  of Parent/Student view filtering, and dedicated Student browser acceptance.
- Blueprint: [M8.9 Student workflow](../../prompts/features/m8-09-student-workflow-acceptance.md),
  **AUTHORIZED — build-feature delegation**, not separate human blueprint review.

College Student is signup intent. Email verification and the existing assigned
independent-eligibility review must precede active self-managed learner/Student
authority. No new automatic role grant, guardian link, self-approved eligibility,
age attestation or independent minor account is introduced. Existing verified
accounts can reach the same setup through Account settings. A valid public
tutor/time continuation survives setup and is revalidated before booking.

College Support is the independent learner level. Reviewed profiles allow the
existing display-name/timezone edits, not unrestricted education-level changes.
Legitimate Parent+Student accounts retain both grants; each role-specific booking
view must show only its corresponding self/guardian records and learner options.
Tutor assignment and all current ownership/evidence/policy checks remain.

Private fictional operation and payments-disabled scope remain as selected in the
[audience decision](2026-10-16-private-fictional-staging.md). No real eligibility
or consent policy, checkout, public launch or hosted activation is approved here.
Booking requests remain provisional until assigned-admin confirmation; assigned
Tutor completion after the saved end remains an administrative record.

Browser completion may use a clearly separated already-ended synthetic historical
booking for the same admitted Student/Tutor. This permits testing the real Tutor
completion action and Student history without altering the new future booking
or shortening policy time. It is not evidence of a new lesson having elapsed,
video attendance or a real Zoom/Meet connection. Actual results belong in the
progress tracker and Student QA report, not in this decision.
