# Local synthetic support reporting

Date: 2026-10-11. Status: authorized delegated local product decision.
Authority: AUTHORIZED — build-feature delegation. The user delegated in-scope choices to the supervising coordinator; coordinator explicitly authorized the recommended workflow through Orca orchestration. No separate human blueprint review or founder-approved incident policy is claimed.

The local demo accepts general/account/safety cases with a subject and fictional description. Requesters submit/read only. Status moves open → in_review → closed, with closed → in_review reopening, only by a currently assigned preauthorized reviewer. Trusted owner fixture tooling alone grants/revokes support capability and assigns/reassigns an exact case, with safe audit history. Reviewers need the current active verified synthetic admin account, support capability and exact assignment; admin alone is insufficient. A reviewer cannot review their own report. Runtime cannot change capabilities or assignment and never browses private learners through this feature.

Rationale: this demonstrates complete case-specific access with the smallest workflow, without introducing runtime staff authority or real incident operations. Alternatives considered were reviewer reassignment and a general admin queue; fixture-only control minimizes authority and disclosure. No reply/chat or internal notes are included. Assignment/revision/status/event writes are transactional; stale revisions and concurrent edits are rejected. Submission uses a requester-owned UUID for retries and a serialized technical limit of five new cases per five minutes. Lists page by twenty; text/body/JSON limits protect this local demonstration without setting a real support policy.

Signed-out contact/report pages expose the local disclosure and sign-in entry. Writes/reads require active verified synthetic accounts, consistent with existing guards. All surfaces identify fictional local testing, not monitored and not an emergency channel. Production/staging are disabled. General reports stand alone; booking references await an actual integration contract.

Human incident/escalation responsibility, operating process, consent/guardian proof, jurisdiction/legal/retention, real staff provisioning/MFA and suspended-user alternative contact remain OPEN launch gates. This technical demonstration does not satisfy those gates or establish emergency guidance, response times, live monitoring or legal facts. No cloud, external messages/email, real users, money or production changes are authorized.
